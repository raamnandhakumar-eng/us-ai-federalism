# Federal Floor or Federal Ceiling?

## Policy brief template

**Question.** Would federal preemption reduce regulatory fragmentation without removing important state-level AI protections?

**Evidence base.** [Number] enacted statutes, [number] states, [number] verified obligation rows, frozen on [date].

**Main result.** [Insert population-weighted protection change with uncertainty or sensitivity range.]

**What a ceiling standardizes.** [Insert domains with the largest reduction in state heterogeneity.]

**What a ceiling removes.** [Insert domains with the largest loss of verified protection coverage.]

**What a floor adds.** [Insert geographic gaps closed by the simulated federal minimum.]

## Policy options

| Option | National consistency | State protection retained | Main implementation question |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Broad ceiling | High | Low by construction | Which state domains are displaced? |
| Targeted ceiling | Medium to high | Depends on carve-outs | How are conflicts and equivalent protections defined? |
| Federal floor | Medium | High | Which minimum duties and enforcement rules apply nationally? |
| Cooperative framework | Medium | High | Who certifies equivalent state regimes? |

## Interpretation limits

The estimates describe statutory coverage and rule heterogeneity. They do not estimate compliance costs, innovation effects, safety outcomes, legal validity, or causal welfare effects.
