# Policy scenarios

## Scenario A: current state system

Retain every verified obligation active on the analysis date. Report future-effective rules separately.

## Scenario B: broad federal ceiling

Set covered state obligations to zero within domains selected by the analyst. This is a deliberately strong benchmark. It does not claim that proposed federal legislation or a court would produce this outcome.

## Scenario C: EO 14365 carve-out ceiling

Apply the broad ceiling except to the order's expressly listed categories:

- Child safety protections
- AI compute and data-center infrastructure, except generally applicable permitting reform
- State government procurement and use of AI

The mapping from a statute to a carve-out must be visible in the released data and reviewed by a human.

## Scenario D: federal floor

For every state-domain cell, replace existing strength with:

`max(state strength, federal minimum strength)`

This measures the coverage created by a national minimum while allowing stricter state rules. It does not model enforcement capacity or behavioral compliance.

## Interpretation

The ceiling scenarios quantify the public protections exposed to displacement. The floor scenario quantifies geographic gaps closed by a minimum standard. Neither measure alone establishes the socially optimal allocation of federal and state authority.
